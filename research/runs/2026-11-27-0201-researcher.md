## 3. What the results teach

1. **The filter only changes trades in the hours when releases happen.** Asia and London trades are identical in eval 1
   and eval 3, and they reproduce R017's ~+0.9-1.0 pips. The whole improvement over R017 (+1.02 to +1.49) comes from 156
   overlap-session trades at +5.27.
2. **The 120-min dose-response killed it.** If post-release flow were the dilution, a longer exclusion should keep or
   raise the edge. Instead it removed the ~70 overlap trades in the 60-120 min band after releases, and the edge fell back
   to R017's level. Eval 1's gain came from one narrow time band, about 1-2 hours after the US 08:30 release. Nothing in
   the mechanism predicts that band.
3. **News-driven excursions also revert** (+1.17, but t 0.8 on 263 trades). The prediction "news moves don't revert" is
   not supported, though the test is too noisy to reject it outright.
4. Conclusion: R034 eval 1's t 3.3 is very likely the selection luck of one window choice. It is not a no-news effect. The
   ledger-wide picture stands: high-vol M15 reversion is about +1.0 pips pre-cost against about 1.05-1.08 cost.
