## What the result teaches

1. **No Tokyo-fix USD demand in EURUSD 2018-21.** The pre-fix short leg is wrong-signed with |t| > 3: EURUSD tends to
   *rise* 08:00-09:54 JST, which is the opposite of the hypothesis. Possible readings: the fix demand is USDJPY-specific
   (USD bought against yen, which need not move EURUSD), or the JST early-morning EURUSD
   drift is a different effect (e.g. Asian buying of EUR; Breedon-Ranaldo would predict foreign-currency buying in
   Asian hours). Not registered as a flip: it is post hoc, and +0.71 would still sit below the ~1.15 pip cost.
2. **Gotobi days look a little more like the story** (post-fix long +1.09, t 1.8, positive 3/4 years) but this is one
   slice of a 553-trade test, below cost (net -0.02 for the long leg alone) and nowhere near t 3. Noted, not iterated.
3. **Thirteen ideas, same pattern.** Every EURUSD intraday mechanism tested sits at |pre-cost edge| <= 1.5 pips per
   trade; the only |t| >= 3 effects are wrong-signed (R006 limit adverse selection, now R013's pre-fix leg). Fix-flow
   effects documented in the literature (London R003/R008, Tokyo R013) are not visible in EURUSD at a tradable size.
