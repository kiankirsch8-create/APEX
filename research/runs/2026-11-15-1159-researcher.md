## Ideas and why

Ledger so far: minute-scale reversion is real but tiny (finding 1); limit fades are adversely selected (R001a, R002a,
R006); hour/session-scale *fades* are null or wrong-signed (R003 -1.14 t -2.1, R005 -1.18 t -0.9); time-of-day drifts

## What each result teaches

1. **No intraday momentum into the NY afternoon** (R009). The sign is positive but t 0.9, and years alternate
   (+1.5, -0.5, -0.6, +1.5). The 3h std (~15 pips) means even a true +0.5 pip effect would need ~8000 trades to reach
   t 3. Combined with R003/R005 (fades of hour-scale moves lose ~1 pip, t <= 2), the honest reading is that
   EURUSD hour-scale returns are close to unpredictable from the day's prior move in either direction.
2. **London-morning breakouts of the Asia range are ~0, not negative** (R010). That fits the mechanism weakly
   (finding 2's reversal was an Asia/late-NY effect and is absent here) but there is no continuation worth trading.
   2019 alone is strongly negative (-2.2, t -2.5) while 2020/21 are +1.3-1.7; that is regime noise, not a filter to
   add. The long/short asymmetry (t 1.6 / -0.8) is within noise and would be a post-hoc cut.
3. **Ten ideas, one pattern.** Every EURUSD intraday effect in the ledger has |pre-cost edge| <= 1.5 pips with
   |t| < 2 except two adverse-selection losses (R006, finding 1's sub-pip reversion). The gate needs roughly
   edge >= 1.35 pips AND edge/std >= 3/sqrt(N). For hour-scale holds (std 15-30 pips) and ~1000 trades that is
   ~1.4-2.8 pips of edge per trade, which no tested mechanism comes near on this single pair.

## Harness / implementation notes (not patched)

- R010's registered rules say orders are "re-sent each bar until 10:00 (same levels)"; the implementation places them
  once at 07:00 and leaves them resting until 10:00 (same levels, same OCO and cancel time). Equivalent in effect;
  documented in the strategy docstring.
- `by_session` uses fixed UTC buckets, so a DST-aware 07:00 London entry shows up as "asia 22-07" in summer, and
  R009's 13:30 NY entries all land in "ny 16-22". Fine for reading, but session slices are not meaningful for
  local-clock strategies.
- `research/bootstrap.sh` reuses an existing `apex` checkout; this session started from a stale `main` checkout and
  needed `git checkout -B research origin/research`. The task prompt's `git clone` fails in that case.
