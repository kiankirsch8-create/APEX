## What the result teaches

1. **The volatility condition works on the pip edge, but only up to about cost.** Unconditional M15 band reversion
   was +0.32 pips/trade (findings). In the top-quartile vol regime the same family gives **+1.02, positive in all
   four years**, t 2.3 on 1,407 trades. Apart from R004 this is the steadiest positive pre-cost edge in the ledger.
   But it lands at the round-trip cost (1.07), so net is about 0.
2. **The pre-registered dose-response check failed.** Going from the top quartile to the top decile did not raise
   the edge per trade (+1.02 both times). Per unit of volatility the premium *falls*. That contradicts the specific
   Nagel prediction (the premium rises more than proportionally with vol). What is left is the ordinary result: pip
   moves, including the reversal, get larger when vol is higher, and cost stays fixed. That gets the edge to cost, not
   past it.
3. **Why I did not iterate.** The levers that remain (2.5 sd bands, longer holds, Asia-only, short-only) are cuts I
   have now seen the results for. The next eval would also need t >= 3.6. With a ~1 pip edge and this trade count,
   none of them can plausibly reach net +0.30 at t 3.6 without fitting. Closed as registered.
