## 1. Idea and why

| id | idea | built on |
|---|---|---|
| R032 | **London-open opening-burst reversal.** move = close(08:14) - close(07:59) on the stored clock. If \|move\| >= thr, market order against the move at 08:15. TP is a 0.5\|move\| retrace, SL is 1.0\|move\| beyond entry, time stop at 09:30. Eval 1: thr 6 pips (~p60). Eval 2 (pre-registered, runs regardless, dose-response): thr 8 pips (~p80) | Mechanism: at the European open, overnight customer orders are executed into books that are still thin (Ito-Hashimoto 2006 on the activity and spread jump at the open). The imbalance-driven part of the first-15-minute move should be transient, like opening overshoots in equities. Finding 1 says short-horizon reversion is real but small, so this trades only the largest excursions, at the moment the imbalance is greatest. Related ideas (disclosed): R029 faded the 15 minutes *into* each hour stamp, R005 faded the whole Asia drift at the open, R010 traded Asia-range breakouts. None of them conditions on the post-open burst |

Uses the stored clock (= NY wall + 5h = London local time), so stored 08:00 is the London open all year despite the
importer bug. I set the thresholds from the distribution of \|move\| alone, over 1012 weekdays in 2018-21: p50 4.35, p60 5.5,
p70 7.05, p80 8.7, p90 11.6 pips. 8 pips was chosen over 10 so that eval 2 can still clear the 200-trade gate. Sanity run on
2019Q2: 12 trades, no warnings, no code change after it.

Considered and dropped before registration (no evals spent):
- **Friday-afternoon weekend squaring** and **Monday weekend-gap fill**: about 190-200 events in 2018-21, so each fails the
  count gate by design.
- **Daily-range exhaustion fade** (fade new day extremes once the range exceeds 1.3x the 10-day ADR): a weak prior. R024
  (fading unusually high volatility) gave +0.56 at t 0.9, and R016/R021 show that large session moves persist. Too close to
  closed ideas to justify an evaluation.

## 3. What the results teach

1. **The opening burst reverts slightly, but only about half as much as costs.** The edge rises with the threshold
   (0.34 to 0.53), which is the direction the mechanism predicts. Win rate is 64%, consistent with a small real snap-back
   that hits the half-retrace TP often but loses more on the 1:1 stop. At about 1 pip of edge sd per 0.5-pip mean, t stays
   near 1, so the effect isn't resolvable on one pair even if real.
2. **2018 is negative in both evals.** In the 2018 down-trend, opening moves continued, as in R021 and R025. The
   reversion is regime-dependent, not structural.
3. This matches the ledger-wide pattern. Every reversion conditioning so far (volatility, session, trend, fix windows,
   spread, run length, and now the open burst) reaches +0.3 to +1.0 pips pre-cost. Round-trip cost is about 1.0 pip.
