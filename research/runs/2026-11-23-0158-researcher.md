## What the results teach

1. **No London-morning continuation into the fix at this horizon.** The 3h45m forward move after a strong morning is
   indistinguishable from zero (+0.28 pips, t 0.2, sd about 27 pips per trade). The stronger signal is *worse*
   (-1.17), the opposite of the dose-response the execution mechanism predicts. If fix-benchmarked orders are worked
   through the day, their afternoon remainder is not visible against normal 4h noise, or it has been priced by midday.
2. **"M15 runs continue" (R021) does not scale to session moves.** R021's continuation was about 0.5 pips over a few
   bars. At the 4h horizon the effect is gone. That matches R009, R010 and R020. Taken together with R014/R005 (session
   moves don't revert either), EURUSD session-scale moves look close to a random walk at these horizons on HistData.
3. **The short-side asymmetry shows up for the sixth time** (R017, R018b, R020, R022, R024, R025). Longs lose and
   shorts win by about +1 to +2.5 pips pre-cost in every intraday European-hours design. That is R004's
   European-hours EUR depreciation (closed at +0.97, t 1.9), showing up as a side effect, not something the
   conditions add. I did not cut R025 to shorts. Doing that would be fitting a seen slice.
