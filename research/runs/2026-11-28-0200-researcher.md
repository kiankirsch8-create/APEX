## 3. What the results teach

1. **New day extremes do not revert within 4h. If anything they continue.** R036 is negative in all 4 years. This is
   the fourth hour-to-day-scale test pointing the same way (R003 pre-fix moves, R005 Asia drift, R020 next-day
   continuation). EURUSD reversion is a local, minutes-scale effect, and it does not scale up to day-range context.
   The long/short split (buying new lows is worst) matches R014's note that EURUSD drifted up in some windows. It is
   not registered, and I am not flipping R036 post hoc.
2. **R017's reversal happens within the first M15 bar.** R037 traded the same high-vol excursions as R017, waited for
   price to come back inside the band, and earned exactly 0.00 over 1038 trades. So R017's +1.0 pips is earned in the
   first 15 minutes after the signal, and nothing is left once price is back inside the band. That has two consequences:
   - a "confirmation" filter cannot separate liquidity moves from information moves on this signal, because by the time
     the move is confirmed the edge is gone;
   - every idea in this family pays its full ~1.05 pip round trip to capture a move of about 1 pip that is over within one
     bar. Changing exits or holding periods cannot fix that ratio. Only a cheaper entry could, and R022 showed that limit
     entries at the signal mid are adversely selected.
3. The ledger-wide picture stands. High-vol M15 reversal at about +1.0 pre-cost is still the only robust effect, and it
   is about equal to cost.

- R036 is a plain M1 strategy that keeps its own day high/low and day-range history. It resets when the clock jumps
