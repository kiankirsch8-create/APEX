## What each result teaches

1. **Resting limits at an extreme are adversely selected.** Both limit variants (R001a, R002a) have negative or zero
   pre-cost edge. A touch-fill at 2.5 sd or beyond a channel happens disproportionately on moves that keep going
   (R001a shorts: -0.88, t -3.5). The ~0.4 pip cost saving from limits (finding 5) is real (costs 1.16-1.21 vs 1.41-1.43
   for market) but is more than eaten by worse fills. **Limit entries are not a free cost cut for fade strategies;**
   they would need to be placed at a level that is reached *after* exhaustion is confirmed.
2. **Reversion edge does not scale with excursion size.** Close-confirmed fades at 2.5 sd on M15 in Asia (R001b) give
   +0.32 pips, the same as Section A's 2 sd bollinger at M15 (+0.32, t 1.9). Filtering for bigger excursions cut
   trades but not the noise-to-edge ratio. Ranked hypothesis 1 ("trade only when the snap-back is large") did not
   hold in this form. The long/short split (long +1.39 t 3.1, short -0.68) is a slice and mostly reflects
   the 2018-21 drift, not a rule.
3. **No stop-run reversion at M5 in Asia** (R002b: 3022 trades, +0.01). The Donchian negative edge in Section A is not
   concentrated in "poke and reject" events.
4. **Fix fade is wrong-signed.** Large pre-fix moves did not revert over 16:05-17:30 London; edge is negative in all
   4 years (t -2.1). The win rate is 56% only because TP (50% retrace) is closer than SL (100%). The continuation
   reading is NOT registered: flipping a sign after seeing a t ~ 2 result is exactly the trap in the findings.
5. **Cost floor.** Even all-limit entries end up around 1.15-1.2 pips round trip (commission 0.70 + exit spread +
   stop/market exit slippage). Any idea needs pre-cost edge of roughly 1.5+ pips; none of the intraday fades here
   gets within 1 pip of that.

## Next steps (for the next run)

1. **Longer holding horizon, fewer trades.** Every M5/M15 effect tested sits at |edge| < 0.5 pips against ~1.2-1.4
   cost. Edge must come from horizons where typical moves are tens of pips (H1-H4 decisions, holds of hours to a day),
   so cost becomes a small fraction. Candidate mechanisms: daily-session handoff (Asia range vs London continuation),
   month-end/quarter-end rebalancing flow (few trades: check the 200-trade gate first).
2. **R001 last useful test**: limit placed *after* confirmation (close beyond band, then rest a limit at the close
   for one bar) to get market-entry selection with limit cost. Only worth one eval; edge would still be ~0.3.
3. **Cost reality check** remains the blocker for any short-horizon idea (finding 5, hypothesis 5): at 1.2 pips floor
   nothing in this family survives. If the real account has lower commission, re-run R001b before new M15 work.
