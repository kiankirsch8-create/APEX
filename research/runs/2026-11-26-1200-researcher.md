## 1. Idea and why

| id | idea | built on |
|---|---|---|
| R033 | **Information-discreteness ("frog in the pan") continuation.** At M15 decisions in stored 07:00-15:45, if the 4h move R has \|z\| >= 1 (z = R / (sigma_M15 * 4)) and the path is smooth (ID = sgn(R) * (%down bars - %up bars) <= -0.375, i.e. >= 11 of 16 bars in the move's direction), go with the move at market. Hold 4h or until stored 20:00, no TP, catastrophe SL 3 sd. One trade per day. Eval 2 (pre-registered, runs regardless, dose-response): ID <= -0.5 | Da-Gurun-Warachka (2014): limited attention leads to underreaction to information that arrives in many small pieces, so momentum concentrates in low-ID moves. Ledger link (disclosed): R021 showed long one-directional M15 runs continue (fade -0.54, t -3.1, all 4 years), and R026 showed that continuation disappears when the path holds a large counter-bar. So path composition seemed to matter. R009, R020 and R025 conditioned continuation on move *size* only. None conditioned on discreteness at a fixed size |

I set the thresholds from the distribution of z and ID alone (2018-21, no outcomes). Among \|z\| >= 1 bars, ID p10 is -0.5 and p30 is
-0.375. Qualifying days: about 670 at -0.375 and about 350 at -0.5. Sanity run on 2019Q2: 37 trades, no warnings, no code
change after it.

## 3. What the results teach

1. **Smooth 4h moves don't continue over the next 4h in EURUSD.** Both point estimates are about -1 pip on a 4h hold
   whose sd is about 25 pips, so the result is noise around zero or slightly negative. The dose-response pointed the wrong
   way (smoother path gave a worse result), and the sign flips by year. The frog-in-the-pan mechanism doesn't show up at
   this horizon.
2. **R021's run continuation is short-lived.** The only \|t\| >= 3 continuation fact in the ledger (+0.54 after a >= 5-bar
   run) came from a 1:1 bracket around an R/3 target, so most of it is realised within one or two bars. Holding the
   direction for 4h, or entering on the path-smoothness signal instead of the run end, gives nothing. This fits the
   ledger-wide pattern: hour-to-session continuation (R009, R020, R025, R033) is never resolvable, and minute-to-hour
   reversion (finding 1, R017) is real but stays at or below the ~1.05-pip cost.
3. Trades entered in the overlap (12-16) were the worst slice in both evals. That is a seen slice, and I'm not proposing anything from it.
