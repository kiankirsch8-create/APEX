| id | idea | built on |

## 3. What the results teach

1. **No holiday liquidity premium.** Thin-day reversal is +0.34 against +0.24 on normal days. The gap of +0.10 is well
   inside the pre-registered 0.5-pip rejection band, and the sign flips by year. Calendar-proxied dealer capacity doesn't
   move the reversal in EURUSD. On thin days the excursions are also smaller (median 11.6 vs 13.3 pips to the mean), so
   even a real premium would have to be large to clear cost.
2. **The control is a clean baseline worth keeping.** An unconditional M15 2-sd band fade with these exits gives +0.24 pre-cost
   over 5582 trades (t 1.5) in 2018-21. That matches finding 1 (+0.32 at M15) under the current rules and costs. R017's
   high-vol filter lifts this to about +1.0. Volatility is still the only conditioning variable in the ledger that has
   moved the reversal edge materially, and it plateaus at about cost.
3. Thin-day NY 16-22 (+4.17 on 29 trades) is one seen slice of 350 trades. Nothing in the mechanism predicts it, and I'm not acting on it.
