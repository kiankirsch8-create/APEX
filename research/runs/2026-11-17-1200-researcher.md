## Ideas considered and why

**Considered and not registered: liquidity-withdrawal (quote-fade) reversion.** The idea: a mid move during a
spread blow-out is market makers pulling quotes, not trades, so it should revert once quotes come back. Before
registering I checked how often it fires on 2019Q2 (conditions only, no outcomes). M1 bars with mean spread
>= 2x the 60-bar median *and* a body >= 3 pips: **6 in a quarter** (>= 3x: 0). HistData spreads are too coarse and
too tight for this to reach the 200-trade gate. A low-tick-count version is not possible either, because
`MarketData` drops `tick_count` and HistData volume is 0. I did not touch the core files to get it.

**Registered and tested: R016 prior-day high/low stop cascade.**

| id | idea | built on |

| R016 | London/NY hours (07-16 UTC): BUY STOP 2 pips above the untouched prior-day high (17:00 NY daily candle), SELL STOP 2 pips below the prior-day low; SL 6 pips back inside the level, TP 14 pips beyond it, 60 min max hold | Osler (2003, 2005): stop-loss orders cluster beyond salient levels and fire as market orders when crossed, so price accelerates through them. The prior-day high and low are the most watched stop levels. The hours avoid the Asia/late-NY windows where finding 2 shows breakouts reverse. ## What the result teaches

1. **The prior-day high and low carry no stop-cascade continuation** within 60 minutes in London/NY hours. Edge is
   about 0 in every year and both sessions. The side split is within noise (|t| <= 1) and was not registered.
   Taken with R007 (round numbers, about 0), R010 (Asia-range break, about 0) and R002 (4h-channel fade in Asia, about
   0), level-based order-clustering effects are not visible in HistData EURUSD 2018-21 at 1h horizons, whichever
   level and direction you pick.
2. **Fewer trades than the frequency check suggested** (248 against roughly 500). Many first touches of the level
   reach it but not the extra 2 pips, and the rule then marks the level dead. That is the rule as registered. The
   count still clears the 200-trade gate.
3. **Power arithmetic** (for Kian, not a new test). To reach edge t >= 3 with a 1.4 pip edge, a 30-minute hold needs
   about 350 trades and a 4-hour hold about 2,700. One-trade-per-day ideas with multi-hour holds cannot pass the gate
   on four years of one pair unless the pre-cost Sharpe is about 1.5 or higher. That is a large part of why sixteen
   ideas have failed.
