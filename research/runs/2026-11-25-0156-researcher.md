## 1. Idea and why

| id | idea | built on |
|---|---|---|
| R030 | **Daily-horizon time-series momentum.** Every weekday take a sample at the first bar >= stored 12:00. Mon-Thu, if N earlier samples exist in the block, go long or short at market in sign(s_d - s_{d-N}). Hold exactly 24h, to the next weekday's sample bar, with a 150-pip catastrophe SL. No Friday entries. Eval 1: N = 10. Eval 2 (pre-registered, runs regardless): N = 20 | FX time-series momentum (Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012; Menkhoff et al. 2012; Baltas-Kosowski on short-lookback trend following): slow information diffusion plus mechanical CTA flow. All 29 earlier ideas traded minutes to one session (<= 9h). The ledger repeatedly finds that session-scale moves are permanent, not transient (R003, R005, R014, R021 t 3.1). The daily horizon was the one structurally different horizon left untested. At a 24h hold, cost (about 1.05 + 0.8 funding for longs) is small next to the ~50-pip daily sd |

Uses the stored clock only (12:00 stored = 07:00 NY wall all year), so the timezone bug doesn't matter. Data is block-local
(blocks are scored independently with no warmup), so each quarter loses its first N sample days. Sanity run on 2019Q2:
44 trades, all 24h holds, no warnings. No code change after it.

## 3. What the results teach

1. **No daily-horizon trend premium shows up in EURUSD 2018-21.** Both lookbacks have an edge of about -1 pip on a 24h
   hold whose sd is about 50 pips, so t is about -0.5. The sign isn't stable by year. 2018, a strong down-trend
   year, was the worst for the trend rule (-7.6). The trend there ran in a few large steps with daily reversals in
   between, and a 10-day sign doesn't catch that.
2. **The gate can't resolve this family on one pair.** A textbook TSMOM premium of 2-5 pips/day with daily sd ~50 needs
   roughly 900-5000 independent trades for t 3. Block-local data (no warmup) caps the 4-year build at about 680. So
   even a real but modest effect would fail here. The negative point estimate means there is nothing to chase anyway.
3. With R030 the ledger now covers minute, hour, session and day horizons for reversion and continuation alike. Only
   the minute-to-hour reversion (finding 1 / R017: up to +1.0 pips) is reliably non-zero, and it stays at or below cost.
