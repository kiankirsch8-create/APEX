## What the results teach

1. **The day-session move does not unwind overnight** (R014). Both thresholds are about 0 and the larger threshold is
   *worse*, the opposite of what inventory pressure predicts (a bigger push should give a bigger unwind). Together with
   R003 (pre-fix moves continue), R005 (Asia drift does not revert into London), R009 and R011, hour-to-session EURUSD
   moves in 2018-21 look like permanent impact. Finding 1's reversion is a minute-scale effect only.
2. **The side split points to an unconditional overnight up-drift, not a conditional effect.** Buying after down days
   gains +1.6 and selling after up days loses -2.2, which is what a plain ~+1.9 pip EURUSD drift over 22:15-06:00 UTC
   would produce. It agrees in sign with R013's pre-fix leg (EURUSD rose 08:00-09:54 JST = 23:00-00:54 UTC, t 3.15),
   but the windows overlap, so it is one observation, not two. **Not registered:** it was found by looking at build
   results, so any further build test would be fitting. Even at +1.9 pips it would net about +0.5 after the 1.40 Asia
   cost, and the only clean test is out of sample, which is the validator's data. I am noting it for Kian, not
   proposing it as a candidate.
3. **No 10:00 NY pinning to round numbers** (R015). Without strike open-interest data, "nearest 00/50" is a weak proxy
   for where large expiries sit; the result is null in every year and on both sides. R006/R007/R015 together say round
   numbers carry no tradable effect in HistData EURUSD at 1h horizons.
4. **Asia costs more than the profile average.** R014 paid 1.40 pips per round trip versus about 1.05 intraday, from
   wider raw spreads at the post-roll entry and the 06:00 exit. Any future Asia overnight idea should budget about 1.4.
