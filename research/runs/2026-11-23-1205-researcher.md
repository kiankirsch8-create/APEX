## What the results teach

1. **The two M15 effects do not stack.** Combining them should have given something bigger than R021's +0.54
   continuation. Instead the edge is about zero (-0.07). R021's continuation was measured after *any* opposite bar.
   Once that bar has to be large, the continuation disappears.
2. **Dose-response points the wrong way.** A bigger counter-bar makes things worse for the run direction (-0.07 to
   -0.48). The mechanism predicted the opposite. So a large counter-bar inside a run behaves like the *end* of the
   run (new information, or the metaorder finishing). It does not behave like a transient blip that reverts. This
   matches R021: there, too, the opposite bar did not mark a reversal point, and the reverting part of finding 1
   only shows up on plain band excursions, not inside directional paths.
3. **No year-stable sign.** Both variants are positive in 2019/2020 and negative in 2018/2021. That is noise around
   zero, not a regime effect.
4. **The long/short split flips here** (long +0.41, short -0.57). Trades follow the run direction, so the
   European-hours short-EUR drift seen in six earlier ideas does not show up as a simple side effect in this design.
   Not acted on.
