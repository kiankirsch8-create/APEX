## 1. Idea and why

| id | idea | built on |
|---|---|---|
| R029 | **Top-of-hour benchmark pre-positioning reversal.** At each hourly stamp T (stored 07:00-20:00, NY wall 10:00 and 14:00 excluded for US releases), move = close(hh:59) - close(hh:44). If \|move\| >= 8 pips (about p91 of the unconditional distribution, from mids only), fade at market. TP 0.5x move, SL 1.0x move, flat at T+20 min. Eval 2 (pre-registered, runs regardless): also half-hour stamps (BFIX cadence), NY 08:30 excluded | Finding 1 (reversion is real but small). The mechanism is benchmark pre-hedging: dealers pre-hedge fix orders before WMR (hourly) and BFIX (half-hourly) stamps and stop once the fix prints (Evans 2018; Melvin-Prins 2015). So the move into a stamp should be partly transient, and larger than the generic reversion. Not covered by R003/R008/R013, which were single daily fixes. The clock bug doesn't affect minute-of-hour, and the NY exclusions use NY wall time = stored - 5h, which is exact |

Threshold check without outcomes: over 2018-21 weekday stamps at 07-20, there were 21,118 top-of-hour windows (1,970 with
\|move\| >= 8 pips) and 21,330 half-hour windows (1,741 with \|move\| >= 8).

## 3. What the results teach

1. **The intraday fixes show no extra reversion.** Edge into the half-hour stamps alone works out to about +0.41
   pips/trade ((2809 x 0.36 - 1573 x 0.32) / 1236). That is the same as top of hour (+0.32), and about the size of the
   generic M15 reversion in finding 1 and R017/R022/R024 (+0.5 to +1.0). If benchmark pre-hedging pushed price before
   the stamps, the reversion would be concentrated there. It isn't. Pre-hedging at intraday fixes is either too
   small to see in EURUSD or spread over more than 15 minutes. The edge is about 0.7 pips below cost, so this family
   can't pay.
2. **The late-NY slice looks like noise.** "ny 16-22" reaches t 3.0-3.5 in R029, but the other reversion ideas don't
   show it at those hours (all build results checked): R017 +0.60 (t 0.4), R022 -0.08, R024 -0.18, and R021 (a
   run fade) -0.95 (t -2.8). With about 4 sessions x 29 ideas sliced, one t-3 slice is expected by chance.
   Not registered.
3. The two variants share most of their trades, so eval 2's higher t (2.8) mostly comes from the larger sample at the
   same per-trade edge. The edge itself didn't grow.
