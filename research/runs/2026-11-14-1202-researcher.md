## Ideas and why

The previous run's main lesson: every M5/M15 effect sits at |edge| < 0.5 pip against ~1.1-1.4 pip cost, so edge has to come
from multi-hour horizons where typical moves are tens of pips (previous report, next step 1). Both ideas follow that.

## What each result teaches

1. **The local-hours effect looks real in sign but is about 1 pip per 5-6h leg.** Pre-cost edge is positive in every build year
   in all three R004 variants, and moving the US leg out of the London/NY overlap (a -> b) turned the long leg from -0.34 to +0.58,
   which fits the mechanism (in the overlap both regions' flows net out). But the move std over 5-6h (~30 pips) is so large that
   2000 trades only give t ~1.6-1.9, and the edge (~0.9-1.0) is below the ~1.1 pip round trip. The European-hours short is the
   steadier part (+1.4-1.5 pips, t 1.7-1.9). DST-aware clocks did not help (c is no better than b). This is the first idea in the
   loop whose pre-cost edge is close to the cost, but it is not a strategy: it would need roughly 2x the edge per trade.
2. **Large Asia drifts do not revert in the London morning** (R005: -1.18, negative in 3 of 4 years). Shorts after an up-drift are
   the worst slice (-2.1, t -1.3). Combined with R003 (fix moves continue) and R001/R002, the evidence keeps saying that
   reversion in EURUSD lives only at minute horizons and is gone by the hour/session scale. Continuation is NOT registered
   (post-hoc flip on t ~ 1).
3. **Cost check.** On the new profile an intraday market round trip is 1.05-1.15 pips (spread floor 0.7 + slippage 0.2 x 2 on
   market/stop exits). This is the bar every idea has to clear by 0.3+.
