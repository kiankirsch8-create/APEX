## Fidelity verdict
Data needs:
- **Profiles, VAH/VAL/POC, LVN, delta**: TradingView Premium `request.footprint()` gives per-bar, per-price buy/sell
  volume, POC, VAH/VAL and delta, so S2, L1, X1 can be built from real NQ volume. ✓
- **E1/E2 big-order bubbles**: these are **individual aggressive executions of 30+ contracts**. Footprint data is
  aggregated per price row per bar; it does **not** show individual trade sizes. → **MISSING on TradingView.**
  A faithful E1 needs NQ trade-level data with size and aggressor side (Databento trades). Even then, how Deepcharts
  groups a sweep into one "bubble" is not stated → INTERPRETED.
- **He says step 3 can't be automated (10:55), and management (X2, X5, X6) is read live.**

So the best achievable grade is **Proxy**, even with trade data: S/L/X1 are mechanical; E1 can be made mechanical only
with trade data; X5/X6 must be pinned to one definition each. Under §0 this needs Kian's decision before any code.
