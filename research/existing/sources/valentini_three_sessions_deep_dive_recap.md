2. Why He Replaced the 1-Minute Chart with a 40-Range Chart

• The 1-Minute Flaw at the NY Open: During the first 15–30 minutes of the NY cash open, time-based 1-minute candles compress massive institutional volume into single giant bars while alternating with low-volume chop. This visual clutter hides the exact price ticks where institutional absorption occurs. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DyS79Eb92Ug)
• How the 40-Range Chart Solves It:
	• A 40-Range bar ignores time completely and prints a new candle only when price travels 40 ticks (10 points on NQ) from high to low.
	• During explosive volatility, a single 1-minute candle might split into four or five 40-Range bars, exposing every micro-pullback, Low Volume Node (LVN), and absorption cluster along the leg.
	• During dead chop, fewer bars print, filtering out time-based noise.

3. Spot Two-Sided Absorption: Instead of buying a blind limit order at support, he watches executed market orders (bubbles) on Deep Charts. 4. Enter via Buy-Stop Order (Never Catching a Falling Knife): To ensure buyers have actually taken control, Fabio places a buy-stop order just above the absorption bar/cluster. Price must tick upward and trigger him into momentum.

Fabio evaluates NQ using four sequential order-flow filters: [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUYBdYnXUNc&vl=en&t=1392), [2] (https://youtubesummary.com/summary/HUYBdYnXUNc)
1. Value Migration (Directional Compass):
	• Track how the intraday Value Area (VAH, VAL, and POC) develops over time.
	• Bullish Value Migration: Consecutive Value Areas and POCs step steadily higher.
	• Bearish Value Migration: Consecutive Value Areas and POCs step steadily lower.
	• Overlapping Value: Sideways equilibrium (no trend edge). [1] (https://youtubesummary.com/summary/HUYBdYnXUNc), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUYBdYnXUNc&vl=en&t=1392)
2. Path of Least Resistance & Order-Book Thinness:
	• Fabio measures effort vs. result: if buyers can push NQ up 20 points using fewer contracts than sellers needed to push it down 5 points, the sell-side order book is thin—creating an explosive path of least resistance upward. [1] (https://youtubesummary.com/summary/HUYBdYnXUNc)
3. Delta vs. Price Absorption:
	• Compare net Delta (aggressive market buy orders minus market sell orders) against actual price displacement.
	• Opening Red Cluster Signal: If the NY session opens with a heavy red sell-delta cluster that fails to break lower, sellers are trapped and absorbed—marking a high-probability long zone. [1] (https://youtubesummary.com/summary/HUYBdYnXUNc), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUYBdYnXUNc&vl=en&t=1392)
4. Proprietary "NASDAQ Effort" Large-Order Layer:
	• Layer filtered institutional prints (big order bubbles) on top of Delta and Value Migration. You only pull the trigger when all layers align in the same color/direction and a candle closes confirming control.
