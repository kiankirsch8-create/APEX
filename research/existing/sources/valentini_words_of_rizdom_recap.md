5. Core Strategy & Order-Flow Mechanics Explained (29:30 – 01:17:00)

• Order Flow vs. Price Action ("Driving with Google Maps vs. Looking Through the Windshield"):
	• Price action is the effect/result; order flow is the cause (actual executed buy/sell orders).
	• Trading purely with price action is like driving while staring only at Google Maps—you know the route, but you can't see traffic jams (thick passive limit walls) or open roads (thin order books). Order flow reveals why a level holds: not because of "conspiracy stop hunts," but because aggressive market orders are being absorbed by massive passive limit orders.
• Why Fabio Targets Consistent 3R–5R Instead of 1:20 "Home Runs":
	• Statistics show markets spend ~70% of the time in stationary/consolidation regimes and only reach extreme VWAP standard deviations ~10% of the time (or full range-to-range expansions ~20% of the time).
