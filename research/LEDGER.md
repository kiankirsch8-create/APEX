### R004: Local-trading-hours depreciation (Breedon-Ranaldo intraday seasonality)

- **Mechanism:** Breedon & Ranaldo (2013, JMCB 'Intraday patterns in FX returns and order flow'): currencies systematically depreciate during their own local trading hours, because domestic agents (importers, investors buying foreign assets, hedgers) are net buyers of foreign currency when their home market is open, and liquidity providers absorb that predictable flow and are compensated by the subsequent price move. For EURUSD this means EUR weakens during European hours and USD weakens during US hours. This is a structural, non-discretionary flow effect at a multi-hour horizon (typical 5-7h range 20-40 pips), so the ~1.05 pip round trip is a small fraction, unlike every M5/M15 idea so far (findings 1/5, R001 run next-step 1). Intraday only, so no overnight funding.
- **Hypothesis:** Short EURUSD 07:00->12:00 UTC and long EURUSD 13:00->20:00 UTC every weekday: combined pre-cost edge >= 1.5 pips/trade, net >= +0.3, edge t >= 3, both legs positive, positive in >= 3/4 build years

US leg: at first bar >= 13:00 and < 13:30, MARKET BUY, close at first bar >= 20:00. Skip entry if spread > 2 pips. Catastrophe SL 50 pips, no TP. One position at a time, one entry per leg per day.

### R006: Round-number take-profit clustering: fade the first approach to a 00/50 level

### R009: Intraday time-series momentum into the NY afternoon

Two non-discretionary channels: infrequent rebalancers/late-informed traders who act near the close in the direction of the day's news (Bogousslavsky 2016), and dealers/hedgers squaring inventory and gamma before the 17:00 NY roll in the direction of the day's move. For EURUSD the FX day starts 17:00 NY; once Europe leaves (after ~12:00 NY) the remaining US-afternoon flow should extend the day's move. Horizon is 3h (std ~10-15 pips), so cost is a modest fraction, and it is sign-symmetric (not the unconditional R004 drift). At first bar with NY time in [13:30, 14:00) whose ref is from the previous calendar day (Sunday for Monday) and spread <= 2 pips: r1 = close - ref; if |r1| >= 15 pips MARKET order in sign(r1). Catastrophe SL 30 pips, no TP. Close at first bar with NY time >= 16:30 (before the 22:00 UTC funding roll) or if the NY date changes. One trade per day.

### R010: London-open breakout of the Asia range (informed European order flow)

The Asia range (00:00-07:00 London) is built in thin, low-information inventory trading. A break of that range during the London morning should mostly be informed flow and continue, unlike breakouts in Asia/late NY, which finding 2 shows reverse (Donchian worst in Asia 22-07 UTC and late NY).
