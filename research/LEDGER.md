### R004: Local-trading-hours depreciation (Breedon-Ranaldo intraday seasonality)

- **Mechanism:** Breedon & Ranaldo (2013, JMCB 'Intraday patterns in FX returns and order flow'): currencies systematically depreciate during their own local trading hours, because domestic agents (importers, investors buying foreign assets, hedgers) are net buyers of foreign currency when their home market is open, and liquidity providers absorb that predictable flow and are compensated by the subsequent price move. For EURUSD this means EUR weakens during European hours and USD weakens during US hours. This is a structural, non-discretionary flow effect at a multi-hour horizon (typical 5-7h range 20-40 pips), so the ~1.05 pip round trip is a small fraction, unlike every M5/M15 idea so far (findings 1/5, R001 run next-step 1). Intraday only, so no overnight funding.
- **Hypothesis:** Short EURUSD 07:00->12:00 UTC and long EURUSD 13:00->20:00 UTC every weekday: combined pre-cost edge >= 1.5 pips/trade, net >= +0.3, edge t >= 3, both legs positive, positive in >= 3/4 build years
- **Rules:** M1 decisions, UTC clock, weekdays. Europe leg: at first bar stamped >= 07:00 and < 07:30, MARKET SELL, close at first bar >= 12:00. US leg: at first bar >= 13:00 and < 13:30, MARKET BUY, close at first bar >= 20:00. Skip entry if spread > 2 pips. Catastrophe SL 50 pips, no TP. One position at a time, one entry per leg per day.
