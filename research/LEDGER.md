### R011: Unscheduled M1 liquidity-shock reversal

### R014: Overnight Asia unwind of the European/US day-session move

### R015: 10:00 NY option-cut pinning toward the nearest 00/50 strike

- **Mechanism:** Option pinning (Ni, Pearson & Poteshman 2005 JFE; Avellaneda & Lipkin 2003): when dealers are net long gamma in options expiring at a strike, their delta hedging sells rallies above and buys dips below it, pulling spot toward the strike as expiry approaches. EURUSD vanilla options expire daily at the 10:00 New York cut and strikes with large notional cluster at round numbers (00 and 50). The counterparty is mechanical delta-hedging flow, which stops at the cut. So in the hour before 10:00 NY spot should drift toward the nearest 00/50 level. Not the same as R006/R007 (fade or follow a first touch of an untouched level, any hour, limit/stop at the level): this is a market entry from a distance, timed to the expiry clock, ending at the cut.
- **Hypothesis:** Weekdays, when at 09:00 NY spot is 4-12 pips from the nearest 0.0050 multiple, a market trade toward it held to the 10:00 NY cut (TP at the level): >= 250 trades, pre-cost edge >= 1.5 pips/trade, net >= +0.3, edge t >= 3, positive in >= 3/4 build years.
- **Rules:** M1 decisions, America/New_York clock, weekdays. At first bar with NY time in [09:00,09:15) and spread <= 2 pips: L = nearest multiple of 0.0050 to close; dist = |close - L|; if 4 <= dist <= 12 pips, MARKET order toward L with TP = L and SL = entry -/+ 12 pips (away from L). Close at first bar with NY time >= 10:00 or on NY date change. One trade per day.
- **Status:** closed
- **Closed (2026-11-17):** 1 eval as registered: 313 trades, edge -0.22 (t -0.4), years -0.0/+0.8/-0.3/-1.1, both sides ~0. No pull toward the nearest 00/50 level in the hour before the 10:00 NY cut; without strike open-interest data the round-number proxy carries nothing.

### R016: Prior-day high/low stop cascade in London/NY hours

- **Mechanism:** Osler (2003 JF; 2005 JIMF): stop-loss orders cluster just beyond salient price levels and, when triggered, execute as market orders in the direction of the cross, so prices accelerate after crossing them. ### R017: Volatility-scaled liquidity-provision reversal (Nagel 2012)

- **Mechanism:** Short-term reversal is the return to liquidity provision: market makers absorb liquidity-demand order flow into inventory and are paid by the subsequent snap-back. Nagel (2012, RFS 'Evaporating liquidity') shows the expected reversal return rises sharply with market volatility, because liquidity providers' risk-bearing capacity shrinks and the required inventory premium grows (also Hendershott-Menkveld 2014; Brunnermeier-Pedersen 2009). Finding 1 says EURUSD M5/M15 band reversion is real (t 3-7) but ~0.2-0.3 pips/trade against ~1.05 cost; the cost is fixed in pips while the reversal premium scales with volatility (more than proportionally per Nagel). So trading the same signal only in the high-volatility regime should raise edge per trade toward/above cost. Market entries after the bar closes (R001/R006: resting limits are adversely selected).
- **Hypothesis:** M15, high-vol regime (trailing 1-day sd of M15 close changes >= 5.4 pips, ~top quartile of build-period values, threshold fixed from the conditioning variable's distribution only, no outcomes): >= 200 trades, pre-cost edge >= 1.5 pips/trade, net >= +0.3, edge t >= 3, positive in >= 3/4 build years. Dose-response check (pre-registered eval 2): top-decile threshold 6.8 pips gives a LARGER edge per trade than eval 1; if not, the mechanism is not operating.
- **Rules:** M15 decisions on completed bars, Mon-Fri, skip entries 20:45-23:15 UTC, spread <= 2 pips. vol = sd of last 96 M15 close-to-close changes; require vol >= 5.4 pips (eval 2: >= 6.8). Band: mean/sd of last 20 M15 closes; if close <= mean-2.0*sd MARKET BUY, if close >= mean+2.0*sd MARKET SELL. TP = mean at signal; SL = entry -/+ (|close-mean|) i.e. 1:1 distance beyond entry; close at market after 8 M15 bars (2h). One position at a time.
- **Status:** closed
- **Closed (2026-11-18):** 2 evals as registered: vol>=5.4 1407 trades edge +1.02 (t 2.3) net -0.04, positive edge all 4 years (+0.8/+1.2/+1.2/+1.2), posQ 59%; pre-registered dose-response failed: top decile vol>=6.8 edge +1.02 (t 1.2), not larger. Reversal edge in pips rises with vol (vs ~0.3 unconditional M15) but plateaus and stays at ~cost (1.06); Nagel-style super-proportional premium absent. Next eval would need t>=3.6.

### R020: Next-day European-session continuation of large prior day-session moves

### R021: Metaorder impact decay: fade the transient part of long one-directional M15 runs

- **Mechanism:** Large liquidity demanders split metaorders into child orders over hours (TWAP/VWAP execution), which shows up as runs of same-sign M15 closes. Finding 2 (short-horizon breakouts reverse, Donchian edge negative) is consistent with transient impact. The first opposite-sign M15 bar after a long run is an observable proxy for the end of the metaorder. Not tested before: R002 faded single stop-runs beyond a channel, R014 session-scale unwinds, R017-R019 band excursions regardless of path; none conditions on a persistent one-directional run.

Pre-registered eval 2 (dose-response, run regardless): run >= 7 bars; square-root impact predicts a LARGER edge per trade than eval 1; if not, the mechanism is not operating.
- **Rules:** M15 decisions on completed bars, Mon-Fri, skip entries 20:45-23:15 UTC, spread <= 2 pips, one position at a time. d_i = close_i - close_{i-1}. At bar t: d_t has sign opposite to d_{t-1}; d_{t-L}..d_{t-1} all strictly the same sign with L >= 5 (eval 2: L >= 7; L counted as the full run length); R = close_{t-1} - close_{t-1-L}, |R| >= 10 pips. ### R022: High-vol band fade with passive limit entry at the signal mid (liquidity provision proper)

- **Mechanism:** R017 found the M15 2-sd band fade in the top-quartile volatility regime earns +1.02 pips pre-cost (t 2.3, positive in all 4 build years) but net -0.04 because a market entry pays the ~0.35 pip half spread plus 0.2 pip slippage.
